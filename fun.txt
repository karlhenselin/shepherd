# What could make the game more fun

Keep the cozy Psalm 23 loop. Fun here is attachment, texture, and little surprises — not more systems.

## Flock feelings

- **Distinct gaits** — Clover glides, Biscuit waddles, Snowball darts, Milo startles. Readable at a glance.
- **Look-back** — A follower turns their head toward you when you stop. Tiny, huge.
- **Name on pet** — You already say their name; vary the lines by personality (Milo: “You’re safe.” Snowball: “Stay close this time.”).
- **Wool fluff** — Pet sheds a couple of floating specks. Cheap delight.
- **Jealous bleat** — Pet one and a nearby sheep baahs once. Comedy, not a mechanic.

## World toy

- **Fire idle** — Sit near the campfire and the shepherd warms their hands. No menu.
- **Water rings** — Stepping the shore makes a one-frame ripple.
- **Birds at dawn** — After the white robe, a short chorus then the wonders track. Marks the new day.
- **Staff tap** — With the crook equipped, a second tap on empty ground clicks the staff (soft wood tick). Sheep glance. Not a weapon.
- **Gem chime** — High, short ding on pickup (in the todo). Make it feel like a find, not an RPG loot sting.

## Pacing

- **Stop the pet pile-on after sleep** — Already started (wake south + move-to-unlock). Do the same after bandage and after loading.
- **One thing at a time** — Never two hint arrows. If thorns and hunger overlap, thorns win until freed.
- **Night is a mood, not a maze** — Wolf at the edge of the camera is enough scare. Don’t add fog that hides the path.

## Moments people will tell someone about

- Finding Clover and the first “The LORD is my shepherd.”
- Bandaging the hole sheep while the others sit aside.
- The howl, the staff, walking anyway.
- Lying down in the gate.
- Waking in white with the flock outside the pen.

Double down on those. New content should set up or echo them, not compete.
